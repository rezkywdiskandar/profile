## Users

Primary users are technical recruiters, engineering hiring managers, tech leads, and prospective clients (founders and enterprise stakeholders) evaluating Rezky Widi Iskandar for Senior Frontend Engineer and UI/UX Specialist roles or high-impact project collaborations.

## Product Purpose

Serve as an authoritative, high-craft personal portfolio and interactive showcase that demonstrates senior-level frontend engineering mastery, design systems expertise, and user experience execution. Success means driving qualified inbound opportunities, interview invitations, and freelance/contract engagements.

## Positioning

Bridging editorial design sensibility with high-performance, accessible frontend engineering. The portfolio proves that technical rigor (fast Core Web Vitals, modular architecture, a11y compliance) and bespoke visual sophistication (polished micro-interactions, editorial typography, clear hierarchy) seamlessly coexist.

## Operating Context

Evaluators review portfolios on both desktop displays (during in-depth candidate reviews and technical screenings) and mobile devices (quick initial scans via LinkedIn or shared links). The site must load instantaneously, offer effortless scannability, and provide interactive proof points (live demos, GitHub repos, credential verification).

## Product Principles

1. **Function Follows Clarity:** Eliminate visual noise so content, case studies, and engineering competence stand out immediately.
2. **Performance as a Feature:** Instant responsiveness, zero-lag transitions, and optimal Core Web Vitals are baseline requirements.
3. **Proof Over Assertion:** Back every skill and claim with verifiable artifacts, live links, clear problem-solution framing, and measurable outcomes.
4. **Editorial Craft:** Treat typography, alignment, contrast, and spacing with pixel-level precision.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Targeting WCAG 2.1 AA/AAA compliance with semantic HTML5 landmarks, full keyboard navigability, robust color contrast ratios, accessible modals with focus management, and reduced-motion support.
